IMDb Ratings for Netflix

Ratings come from the OMDb API. Request a free key there (1,000 lookups a day), activate it from the email they send, and paste it above. The key is from OMDb, not IMDb; IMDb doesn't issue keys. Nothing shows on the video sites until a key is saved.
